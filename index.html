Our Story

دیسی بٹ کڑاہی روات

Authentic Karahi, Mandi & Charcoal BBQ on Main GT Road

Located at T Chowk Rawat in Islamabad, Desi Butt Karahi Rawat (DBK) prepares traditional Desi Chicken and Mutton Karahi cooked to order with optional pure desi butter, alongside authentic Arabian Mandi platters, sizzling Taka Tak, and charcoal BBQ.

Hygienic Kitchens

Fresh meat, clean preparation surfaces, and live cooking for every karahi and grill order.

Spacious Family Seating

Comfortable indoor family halls and outdoor garden seating right on Main GT Road.
